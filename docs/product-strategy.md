# Product Strategy

## Product Type

Modular ERP/POS Platform.

## First Practical Vertical

Fashion Retail.

## Why This Direction

The first real use case is managing a fashion retail business with one branch now and multiple branches later.

The platform should serve the current business first, then become sellable to small and medium fashion brands.

## What We Avoid

- Building a huge generic ERP too early.
- Locking the system to fashion only.
- Starting with microservices too early.
- Building SaaS complexity before the product works in real life.

## Correct Strategy

Build a general ERP/POS core, then add Fashion Retail as the first vertical module.

## Business Stages

### Stage 1

Use the system internally for one fashion retail store.

### Stage 2

Expand to multiple branches.

### Stage 3

Sell the system to small and medium fashion brands.

### Stage 4

Turn it into a SaaS product with subscriptions.

### Stage 5

Add AI, mobile apps, and advanced automation.

## Product Principle

Build for yourself now, but design as if the product will be sold later.
